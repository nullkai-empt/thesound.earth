THESOUND.EARTH — Credits & Provenance

Derived from satellite data. Every note is computed from the data by rules we chose.

Data Sources

NASA PO.DAAC OSCAR Data: Ocean Surface Current Analysis Real-time (OSCAR_L4_OC_FINAL_V2.0), 0.25° resolution. Derived from satellite altimetry and scatterometry.

NASA Goddard Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS): Visualizations SVS 3912, 4950, 5687, 5688.

Audio Rendering & Architecture

Audio Synthesis: FluidSynth sound engine with open-source FluidR3_GM SoundFont.

Music Specification: Deterministic algorithmic sonification pipeline mapping ocean velocity and physical vectors to harmony, rhythm, dynamics, and melody.
