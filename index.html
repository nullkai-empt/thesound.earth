How Data Becomes Sound
Deterministic mapping from physical ocean parameters to acoustic structures
Physical Feature Scientific Parameter Acoustic Rule
Period Mean Speed Mean 95th-percentile surface speed over period Key transposition k = round(6 * (mean_speed - lo) / (hi - lo)) semitones above D. Clamped 0..6. Last bar resolves to tonic minor.
Ageostrophic Flow Non-geostrophic wind-driven flow vector decomposed into 4 quadrants Harmonic chord progression: North (i), East (VI), South (iv), West (VII). Peak section introduces III lift; Calm section introduces v.
Ageostrophic Magnitude Current shear energy threshold (< 0.013 m/s) Harmonic Hold: When energy drops below threshold, previous chord is sustained to prevent artificial churn during slack water.
Block Speed (p95) Current speed normalized over bounds [0, 1] Dynamics & Drums: Velocity scaling (0.65..1.05) and drum groove tier switching (Low < 0.35, Mid 0.35..0.70, High > 0.70).
Calendar Block 7-day aggregated blocks (yearly) or 1-day increments (monthly) 1 block = 1 musical bar at 72 BPM (4 beats per bar, 3.333s per block).
Period SHA-256 Hash Digest of period label ("2025" or "2025-01") Melodic Seed: Deterministic melody contour. Beats 1 and 3 remain strict chord tones.
Honest Limits & Boundaries
Explicit scientific boundaries and deliberate modeling choices
"Every note is computed from the data by rules we chose."
  • Data Encoding, Not Underwater Audio: This music sonifies numerical physical values derived from satellite data. It is not an audio recording of ocean waves, underwater hydrophones, or marine life.
  • 15 Calibrated Regions: Sonification pipelines are calibrated across 15 global regions (7 ocean basins + 8 land wildfire/canopy transects). Clicking the globe snaps to the nearest calibrated region.
  • 2026 Temporal Coverage: Satellite ocean current data for 2026 ends on 16 Jan. Subsequent periods are awaiting satellite processing and calibration.
  • Excluded Earth Domains: Terrestrial vegetation, atmospheric CO2 concentrations, and meteorological wind speeds are not coupled into this sonification engine.
